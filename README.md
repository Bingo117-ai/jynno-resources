# Jynno — Planning Guides & Resources

Resources published by [Jynno](https://jynno.com), an AI creative studio for image generation, photo editing and video creation. Develop a visual direction with a creative Agent, then generate images, edit photos and create videos for product ads and stories.

## Planning guides

- **Plan a Product Visual Before You Generate It** — decide the job the image must do, keep product identity stable across variations, and review composition before sharing.
- **From Product Image to Short Ad Video** — write down the single event the video should show and check product recognizability throughout the clip.
